# User guide (staff)

## Sign in

Open the app → **Login**.

After a clean seed, the initial owner is:

- Email: `owner@crusher.local`
- Password: `ChangeMe@123` (or whatever you set in `OWNER_SEED_PASSWORD`)

Create customers, products, and additional staff under **Users** / master-data screens.

## Daily flow

1. **Dashboard** — today’s sales, production, stock, collections, expenses, dues
2. **Production** — record output, then Post to stock
3. **Sales → New sale** — pick customer, add lines, confirm tunes (×4), transport, vehicle
4. **Post sale** — receives official invoice number
5. **Dispatch** — record truck load; stock reduces on post
6. **Payments** — collect cash/UPI; allocate to invoices
7. **Expenses** — diesel, wages, repairs, etc.

## Internet required

The app is **online-only**. If there is no connection, a banner appears and you cannot save sales or other records until you reconnect.

## Reports

Use **Reports** for daily closing, customer outstanding, stock, and estimated operating result. Export CSV where offered.
